# AdventureWorks Report in Power BI

## Overview
Multi-page business intelligence report built on Microsoft's 
AdventureWorks dataset covering sales, products, and customer 
analytics from 2016-2017.

## Dashboard Pages

### Executive Summary
- Monthly Revenue: $1.83M vs Goal $1.77M (+3.31%)
- Monthly Orders: 2,146 vs Goal 2,165 (-0.88%)
- Monthly Returns: 166 vs Goal 169 (+1.78%)
- Orders by category and subcategory
- Top product by orders: Water Bottle 30oz
- Top product by profit: Mountain-200 Black 46
- Geographic sales distribution across North America, 
  Europe and Pacific

### Product Detail
- Product-level KPI tracking vs targets
- Weekly profit trend analysis
- Price adjustment simulation
- Return rate tracking by product

### Customer Detail
- Top customers by revenue and orders
- Orders by gender, income level and occupation
- Customer age distribution treemap
- Monthly orders and revenue trend

## Key Insights
- Total Orders: 22,534 with 2.14% return rate
- Accessories lead in order volume
- Water Bottle 30oz is highest selling product
- Average income customers drive most orders

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- AdventureWorks Dataset (8 tables)
- DAX measures for KPI calculations
